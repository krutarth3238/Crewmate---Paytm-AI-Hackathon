# Crewmate Design System · Color Palettes Reference

This reference document preserves the design system color palettes. The app is currently running **Palette 1 (Obsidian Cyber-Volt)** as requested, while fully remembering the OG Neo-Brutalist palette for instant switching if desired.

---

## Palette 1: Obsidian Cyber-Volt (CURRENTLY ACTIVE)
* **Canvas Background**: `#080A0F` (Deep Void Black) with subtle linear gradients to `#0B0E17` / `#0A0D15`
* **Primary Brand Accent**: `#1842FF` (Electric Hyper-Cobalt) with glowing box shadows `rgba(24,66,255,0.4)`
* **Vibrant Accent**: `#D8F040` (Cyber Volt-Lime) for primary badges, live dots, and secondary buttons
* **Telemetry**: `#38BDF8` (Sky Cyan) & `#00C853` (Neon Emerald)
* **Cards & Borders**: `#0D111C` and `#0B0E16` cards with `#1B2438` and `#1E2B48` hairline borders
* **Typography**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) in `Bricolage Grotesque` + `#CBD5E1` light slate body

---

## Palette 0: The Original Neo-Brutalist Palette (SAVED IN MEMORY)
* **Canvas Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White)
* **Strokes & Hard Shadows**: `#0D0E11` (Deep Pitch Obsidian) with 2px borders and `shadow-hard-black` (4px/6px solid black offset shadows)
* **Primary Brand Accent**: `#1638F0` (High-Energy Cobalt Blue) — used on primary CTA buttons, clearance tags, and core highlights
* **High-Voltage Contrast Accent**: `#D8F040` (Acid Cyber Lime) — used on selection highlights (`::selection`), active pulse dots, and interactive demo buttons
* **Verified Metric Accent**: `#00C853` (Pure Emerald Green) — used on reliability stats and verified mission checkmarks
* **Subtle Surfaces**: `#EEF2FF` (Soft Lavender-Blue Tint) & `#F9FAFB` (Neutral Offset Pods)
* **Typography Stack**:
  * Display Headings: `Clash Display` & `Syne` (Bold, punchy geometric)
  * Telemetry, Badges, Steppers & Buttons: `Space Mono` (Uppercase, tactile monospaced)
  * Body Text: `Hanken Grotesk` (Clean, high-legibility sans-serif)

---

## Palette 2: Industrial Warm Sand & Ultramarine (Tactile Hardware / Bauhaus)
* **Canvas Background**: `#F6F5F1` (Architectural Warm Sand / Bone)
* **Primary Brand Accent**: `#0E2FE8` (International Klein Ultramarine Blue)
* **Secondary Accent**: `#FF4D26` (Safety Industrial Vermilion) & `#D8F040` for micro-dots
* **Cards & Borders**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards with `#DFDCD4` / `#E3E1DA` warm stone borders
* **Dark Contrast Pods**: Deep Carbon Iron `#14161B` for hardware spec pods and bottom CTA
* **Typography**: Charcoal `#14161B` headings in `Bricolage Grotesque` + `#474B54` slate body copy

---

## Reverting or Switching Palettes
To switch between any of these palettes in the future, simply request:
- *"Switch to Palette 0 (Original Neo-Brutalist)"*
- *"Switch to Palette 1 (Obsidian Cyber-Volt)"*
- *"Switch to Palette 2 (Industrial Warm Sand & Ultramarine)"*
